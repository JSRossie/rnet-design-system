TOKENS · NO NEW PRIMITIVES

The kit adds no new primitives.

Forms & inputs
FIELD · CONTROL · HELP

A field carries three layers: a mono-uppercase label above, the control itself, and an optional help or error message below. Stack vertically with a 6 px gap. The label is the workhorse — it stays mono uppercase even for casual fields so the form reads as instrument, not as a marketing splash.
